Activity recognition

Your network, guessing what you are doing from this iPhone's motion sensors.

1. Lay the phone flat on a table, screen up

Then tap Start and leave it still for a second. The dataset's phone was an Android, and iPhones report gravity the other way round. Lying flat shows which way this one does.

2. Wear it like the dataset's volunteers

  1. On the left side of your waist, in your waistband or a belt pouch
  2. Sideways, screen facing out
  3. Side button pointing up, the top of the phone pointing forward
